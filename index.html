HOW TO PLAY

☀ The Core fires one beam of light. Touch and drag on empty space to aim it.

🪞 Drag a Mirror from the tray into the beam to bounce it. Tap a mirror, then drag its round handle to turn it. Drag it back to the tray to pick it up.

🔺 A Prism splits white light into red, green and blue.

Red burns · Green throws lightning · Blue slows.

🛡 Enemies with a coloured ring can only be hurt by that colour until the ring breaks.

🌑 Every 5th wave an Eclipse comes. It swallows white light and its colour keeps changing.

✹ Kills charge the Flare. Tap it when it glows to blast everything near the core.
